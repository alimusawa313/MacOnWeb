Sandboxy Privacy Policy

Sandboxy is a calm building game for iPhone, iPad and Mac. You draw a little world and walk around in it. None of that needs your personal data, so the game does not collect any.

Last updated 11 November 2026

The short version

There is no Sandboxy account and no Sandboxy server. You never sign in, and you never give us a name or an email address.

Your worlds, photos and videos are saved on your own device. They leave it only when you choose to share or save them.

The game contains no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third party SDK of any kind. It does not connect to the internet to play.

1. What we collect

Nothing. Sandboxy does not send us your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifier, or a record of what you built or how you played. There is no sign up step, because there is no account.

2. Where each thing lives

Everything the game touches, and who can see it:

DataWhere it livesWho can see it
Your worlds (.sandboxy files)On your device, in the game's own storageYou only
Photos, videos and the scrapbookOn your device, in the game's own storageYou only
Settings, tutorial progress and stampsOn your device, in standard app storageYou only
Pictures you save to PhotosYour Photos library, only when you tap saveYou only
Things you shareWherever you send them, only when you tap ShareYou, and whoever you send them to

3. Saving to Photos

When you save a picture or a video from photo mode to your Photos library, your device asks for permission to add to Photos. This is add only access: Sandboxy can put new pictures in your library, but it cannot see, read or change anything that is already there. If you say no, the game still works and your pictures stay in the in-game scrapbook.

You can change this at any time in your device's Settings, under Privacy, Photos.

4. Sharing

You can share a world, a photo or a video with the system share sheet. Nothing is sent until you pick a destination yourself, such as Messages, Mail or AirDrop. Sandboxy does not upload anything on its own, and it never sees what happens after you share. The app or service you choose has its own privacy policy.

5. Ads, tracking and analytics

None. There is no advertising framework, no analytics SDK, no crash reporter and no attribution framework in the game. We do not build a profile of you, we do not track you across apps or websites, and Sandboxy never asks for permission to track because it has no use for it.

The App Store privacy label says Data Not Collected, and that is the whole truth.

6. Apple

You download Sandboxy from the App Store, so Apple handles the download and any payment under Apple's privacy policy. We never see your card details or your Apple ID.

If you have turned on Share with App Developers in your device's Analytics settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and basic usage numbers. These come from Apple, not from the game, carry no name or contact details, and help us fix bugs. You can turn this off in Settings, under Privacy, Analytics & Improvements.

Sandboxy has no in-app purchases today. If any are added, Apple will handle the payment, and this page will be updated first.

7. Children

Sandboxy is a calm game that is fine for all ages. Because the game collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either. There are no chat features, no ads and no links that lead out of the game to buy things.

8. Your rights and deleting data

Depending on where you live you may have the right to see, correct, export or delete the personal data a company holds about you. We hold nothing that identifies you, so there is nothing to hand over and nothing to delete on our side.

Everything Sandboxy saves is on your device. Delete a world or a photo inside the game, or delete the app, and it is gone. If you want to keep a world, share the .sandboxy file to somewhere safe first.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes up on this page with a new date at the top. If we ever add something that collects data, it will be described here first.
