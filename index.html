See & drive the screen

Hardware H.264 up to 120 fps, with full cursor, keyboard, a virtual trackpad, and CompactOS that refits a single Mac app to your phone.

Wake, unlock, 24/7 reach

A power assertion keeps it reachable, a magic packet wakes it, and a 3D padlock unlocks it. Works on the LAN, through a free tunnel, or over iCloud.

Trust model

Off by default. Yours by design.

MacOn is built for one team on machines they own, and treats every remote power as a privilege you grant, not a default.

Pair once, explicitly

Single-use codes with a 15-minute life, rate-limited and verified in constant time. Every device gets its own revocable token.

Every power is a toggle

Screen, control, CompactOS, flows, wake, unlock. All off until you flip them on, per capability, on the Mac itself.

Secrets stay on the Mac

Tokens, API keys and the unlock password live in the macOS Keychain, never in your repo, logs or on the phone.

A tunnel URL grants nothing

Remote access rides HTTPS and WSS through Cloudflare, but the address alone is useless. Pairing codes and bearer tokens are always required.
