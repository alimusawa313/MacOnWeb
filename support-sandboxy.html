Common questions

A few that come up a lot. If yours is not here, just email us.

On your device, inside the game. Every world is a .sandboxy file. To keep a copy somewhere else, or to move a world to another device, open it from the home screen and use Share, then send it to Files, AirDrop or Mail. Opening that file on another device brings the world back.

Yes. Worlds, photos and the scrapbook live on your device only, with no account and no cloud copy. Share any world you want to keep before you delete the app.

Open photo mode, frame your shot and take it, then tap Save to Photos on the print. The first time, your device asks if Sandboxy may add to Photos. If you said no before, turn it back on in Settings, Privacy, Photos, Sandboxy (on a Mac: System Settings, Privacy & Security, Photos).

Sandboxy draws a lot of small details. Very large worlds and full land worlds are the heaviest. Closing other apps helps, and so does letting a warm device cool down. If it stays slow, email us your device model and what you were doing, and we will look into it.

No. The whole game works offline. It has no account, no ads and no online features.

Email help@macon.id. Tell us your device, what you did and what happened. A screenshot or a short screen recording makes fixes much faster.

Payments go through Apple, so refunds do too. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and pick Sandboxy. If anything looks wrong, email us and we will help.

The Sandboxy privacy policy covers it in plain words. In short, the game collects nothing.
