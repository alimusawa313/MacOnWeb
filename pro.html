Questions

Why would I buy on the web rather than in the app?

Mostly you would not, and in the app is simpler. Web checkout exists for people who would rather not put a purchase on their Apple Account, and for regions where a local payment method works better than the App Store.

Does Pro cover both my iPhone and my iPad?

Yes. It is one purchase, and Restore Purchases brings it to any device signed into the same Apple Account.

What happens to my free 40 minutes a day?

They stop mattering. Pro is unlimited remote, so the meter is not counting anything any more. On your own network you were never metered in the first place.

Is the Mac app or the CLI ever paid?

No. The macon CLI is MIT licensed and open source, and the Mac app is free. Pro is only ever about the iPhone and iPad companion.
